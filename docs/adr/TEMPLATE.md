# ADR NNNN: short decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Scope: affected subsystem or contract

## Context

What invariant, failure, or requirement makes this decision necessary?

## Options considered

1. Option A — benefits, costs, and failure modes.
2. Option B — benefits, costs, and failure modes.

## Decision

State the chosen option precisely, including ownership and source of truth.

## Consequences

Record positive consequences, trade-offs, compatibility, migration, and
operational effects.

## Evidence

Name the tests, runtime artifact, fixture, or measurement that validates the
decision. Do not mark the ADR accepted without matching evidence or an
explicitly recorded reason why evidence is deferred.
